# Terms: StochCraft Flow forecast for Jira

Last updated: 21 November 2026.

StochCraft Flow forecast is free, open-source software, licensed under MIT or Apache-2.0 (see `LICENSE-MIT` and `LICENSE-APACHE`). It embeds StochCraft (https://github.com/helenkwok/StochCraft), which has the same licences.

- **Estimates, not promises.** The forecasts come from a simulation built on your project's past issue timings and on assumptions the app states (for example, how many people work at once, which is not in Jira). They show how a flow is likely to behave under stated conditions. They are not guarantees, and you remain responsible for decisions you make with them.

- **Your Jira data stays yours.** What the app reads, and what it does not keep, is described in `PRIVACY.md`.
- **Comments you post.** The **Post to Jira** button adds a comment as you. Check the issue you name before pressing it; you can delete the comment in Jira as you would any of your own.
